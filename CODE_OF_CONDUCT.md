# Contributor Covenant & Dandelion Compact Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and stewards of **Dandelion OS & Embedded AI** pledge to make participation in our open-source commons a harassment-free, accessible, and mutually elevating experience for everyone—regardless of age, physical or sensory ability (including locked-in, deaf-blind, neurodivergent, and assistive-technology users), body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, native language, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community anchored in **Linus's Law** (*"Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow"*), **Robert Pirsig's Quality ($dQ$)**, and the **Dandelion Compact** (*Die Gedanken sind frei*).

## Our Standards

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward other people and learners at every skill level.
* Designing and maintaining features so that all human beings—no matter how limited their sensory or motor channels (`ACCESSIBILITY.md`)—can learn, communicate, and contribute.
* Evaluating competency through **Nested Chromatic Chromatography** (Army After Action Review *Sustains & Improves* across recursive ROYGBIV optical wheels) rather than punitive or middle-clumping metrics.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive, first-principles technical feedback (*Kintsugi Gold `Code 251`*).
