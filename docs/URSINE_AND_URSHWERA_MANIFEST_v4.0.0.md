## 1. The Ursine & Urshwera Anchor: Why We Build

In the engineering of complex computational systems, it is easy to get lost in the machinery—optimizing ternary bitplanes, tuning Linux `PREEMPT_RT` and `sched_ext` latency curves, and managing sparse matrix multipliers. But an operating system without a soul is just a sophisticated electronic tomb.

The **Ursine** (`v4.0.0`) and **Urshwera** (`v4.1.1`) represent our foundational, unyielding invariant: the absolute sanctity of sovereign human intent across the physical boundary layer.

Our ideal user is **Joe Bonham** (*Johnny Got His Gun*). When a human being is stripped of limbs, face, speech, and sound, yet remains completely conscious and locked in behind an impenetrable physical barrier, standard computing offers nothing. **Dandelion OS** was forged precisely for this condition. If our kernel, our `1.25-bit` sparse ternary quantization, and our F-16 Fly-By-Wire (`FBW`) control laws are robust, low-latency, and empathetic enough to establish mutualistic communication with a locked-in user tapping out `S.O.S.` on a pillow, then when you hand that exact same substrate to the rest of the world, it runs with absolute sovereignty, zero bloat, and uncompromising grace.
