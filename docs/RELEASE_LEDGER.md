## Ship-of-Theseus safeguard

Dandelion OS grows by carrying ideas forward, not by pretending its earlier selves never existed. Keep the original epoch names, concepts, and context. Add clarifications alongside them; when correcting a status, say what was corrected and why. Never rewrite a historical milestone simply to make the release page look uniform.

`ACCESSIBILITY.md` remains the project's ethos. Release bookkeeping serves that work; it does not define or replace it.
