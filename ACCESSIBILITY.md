# Dandelion OS v3.2.0 Universal Sensory Accessibility (Bite 10 LIS)
Inspired by *Johnny Got His Gun* and Metallica's "One", Dandelion OS v3.2.0 natively supports Bite 10 (`MICRO_BITE10_SENSORY_LIS = 2001`) Locked-In Syndrome (LIS) single-switch Morse (`morse_to_ternary_bridge`) and MOTU M4 96 kHz / 64-sample (`0.66 ms`) `io_uring` continuous-voltage-to-trit conversion across `{-1, 0, +1}`.
